The person behind the lab.La persona detrás del laboratorio.

Juan Rodríguez Monti is the founder and CEO of Deauty Labs. Over two decades he has led engineering teams and architected cloud-native, security-focused systems — from leading software engineering for a team in Brussels to founding Fibona Software in Buenos Aires. He has taught computer science at UNNOBA, written for Linux Magazine and ACM, and organized Python conferences across two continents. He is the author of Complex Systems at Scale.
